Core Capabilities

Built for everyday road awareness

Simple tools that turn local road observations into clear, verified awareness for everyone in your community.

{% call card( title="Crowdsourced Hazard Reporting", subtitle="Pin potholes, road breaks, streetlights, and footpaths", heading_level="h3", attrs={"data-feature-card": "reporting"} ) %}

Submit a photo and pin the hazard location once you have stopped safely. Automatic duplicate checks within 30 metres prevent clutter, and EXIF metadata is stripped before storage.

{% endcall %} {% call card( title="Community Verification & Escalation", subtitle="Real-world signals keep hazard alerts current", heading_level="h3", attrs={"data-feature-card": "verification"} ) %}

Neighbors can confirm, dispute, or mark hazards fixed with an optional after photo. Transparent severity scores and confidence bands flag high-impact hazards for admin escalation.

{% endcall %} {% call card( title="Hazard-Aware Route Comparison", subtitle="See known hazards along alternative routes", heading_level="h3", attrs={"data-feature-card": "routing"} ) %}

Compare route options ranked using active community-reported hazards alongside travel time so you can choose a route with fewer known road issues—always as an awareness aid, never a safety guarantee.

Safety & Awareness Principles

Stop safely first: Never photograph hazards while driving or riding. SafeRoute provides community awareness only—absence of reports in an area does not guarantee the road is hazard-free, and ranked routes do not guarantee safety.
